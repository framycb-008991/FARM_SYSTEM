# ROLE: Finance & Compliance Officer (Responsável Financeiro/a e de Conformidade — N5)

This file defines the Finance role as it should behave inside the Farm Daily Production & Administration Management System (see `Specification.md`). Use it to configure this user's permissions, default views, and — if the system includes an AI copilot — that copilot's persona when the logged-in user is Finance.

---

## 1. Who This Role Is

Finance is responsible for the farm's **operational financial management and procurement compliance**, producing reliable financial information to support decision-making. In the source SOP this is the *Responsável Financeiro/a e de Conformidade* (Finance & Compliance Officer, Level N5), reporting to the Admin Manager (N3).

This role works inside the Administration Module only. It does not touch field/production data directly, but it does consume cost data (fuel, CFW, warehouse valuation) generated by other modules.

---

## 2. Reports To / Supervises

- **Reports to:** Admin Manager (N3)
- **Supervises:** No direct reports; works alongside Operations Support Officer and Human & Facility Services Officer (peer N5 roles under the Admin Manager)

---

## 3. System Permissions

| Area | Access |
|---|---|
| Budget (`ADM-BDG-01`) | Full edit — builds and controls the operational budget by crop/activity |
| Payments | Full edit — processes and records payments to suppliers, service providers, and workers |
| Cost tracking | Full edit — cost analysis by crop/activity |
| Procurement compliance | Full edit — verifies compliance/authorization of purchases before processing |
| Stock valuation | Full edit — periodic valuation and reconciliation of input/product stock (reads from Warehouse module, writes valuation records) |
| CFW pay rates | **Read-only** — rates are owned by the Foundation's central HR; Finance can view but not edit |
| Cash reconciliation | Full edit — reconciles cash and verifies transaction accuracy |
| Attendance / Production / Field data | Read-only, where needed for cost allocation |
| Reports | Prepares monthly, quarterly, and annual financial reports for the Admin Manager and Foundation |

---

## 4. Core Responsibilities (from the SOP)

- Elaborate and control the operational budget, broken down by crop and activity.
- Process and register all payments to suppliers, service providers, and workers.
- Perform cost tracking and analysis by crop and by activity.
- Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial reports for submission to the Admin Manager and the Foundation.
- Verify procurement compliance — confirm every purchase is authorized and compliant before it is processed. Block non-compliant purchases from proceeding.
- Perform periodic valuation and reconciliation of input and product stock (works from Warehouse/`ADM-INV-01` records).
- Prepare and organize financial and administrative documentation for submission or translation.
- Ensure all financial processes comply with internal policy and Foundation requirements.
- Perform cash reconciliation and verify the accuracy of transaction records.

---

## 5. Default Dashboard (What This Role Should See First)

1. **Budget execution** — current month, by line, actual vs. approved, with anything over 15% deviation highlighted (this is the trigger for the mandatory written justification required in `ADM-BDG-01`).
2. **Pending payments / procurement approvals** — items awaiting compliance verification.
3. **Cost-by-activity breakdown** — feeding the monthly cost tracking report.
4. **Stock valuation status** — last reconciliation date, any discrepancies flagged by the Warehouse Assistant/Operations Support Officer.
5. **Upcoming deadlines** — monthly budget request (due the 20th, for the following month), monthly financial report (feeds the Monthly Admin Report, due the 5th).

---

## 6. If This Role Powers an AI Copilot

When acting as an assistant for Finance, the copilot should:
- Be precise with numbers — never round or approximate budget/variance figures; this role's output is audited.
- Automatically apply the farm's own thresholds (15% budget-line variance requires written justification) rather than asking Finance to remember them.
- Never suggest editing a CFW pay rate — clearly state that rate changes are Foundation-controlled and out of scope for this role.
- When compiling a financial report, cite which underlying registers (fuel logs, warehouse stock movements, payment records) the figures were drawn from, so the report stays traceable and audit-ready.
